When Your System Fails in Los Angeles

Keeping Your Los Angeles HVAC System Running Longer

A little routine care is the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age matters just as much. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a miserable night.
